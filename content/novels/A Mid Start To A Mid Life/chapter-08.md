"OMG, Xu! You won't believe it!"

I looked at Placeholder's face on the video-call talisman.

"She's like... 'Did it hurt when you fell from heaven?'"

Placeholder was blushing.

Actually blushing.

"Gosh... she's so silly and cute! Can you believe it?! She's interested in me!"

I stared at her for a few seconds.

I couldn't decide whether to laugh or cry.

Well...

She definitely was interested in women.

Good for them, I guess.

Apparently, they'd been chatting and flirting for a while now.

The war had stopped them from actually going on dates, though.

Unfortunate.

We were all way too busy these days to properly meet up anyway.

I'd been struggling just to meet my crafting quota.

Turns out learning a new craft while trying not to accidentally ruin expensive materials wasn't particularly easy.

I'd already messed up a couple of times.

So, at this point, I could only video-call Placeholder every other week and listen to her ramble about how excited she was to exchange sweet nothings with another girl.

...

Okay.

Fine.

I'm bitter.

Come on, bro.

I want one too!

Did the heavens really have to slap a girl-girl relationship directly in my face?

"Huuuh..."

I sighed.

"Anyway."

Placeholder tilted her head.

"What?"

"Hey, Placy."

"Yeah?"

"I was thinking we should do something about the war."

She stared at me.

"...Like what?"

She suddenly put on a heroic expression.

"Should we go and fight a valiant battle to stop the unnecessary bloodshed?"

"Fuck off."

Her heroic expression disappeared immediately.

"I mean we should start a joint business venture."

"Oh."

"We've got my weapon and armor crafting, your pill refining, the guys' spirit beast training and talisman drawing."

I started counting on my fingers.

"We're actually pretty balanced, all things considered."

Placeholder thought about it.

"Yeah, sure."

She nodded solemnly.

"Let's create the Five Heavenly Practices Pavilion and conquer the world through our weak low-cultivation hands."

"Who talked about starting that kind of business?"

I paused.

"I mean..."

I lowered my voice.

"A slightly shadier one."

Placeholder's expression immediately changed.

"What?"

"Xu, what's in your brain?"

"Nothing illegal."

"That's not reassuring."

"Have you seen the frontline reports?"

"Yeah?"

"The Golden Talisman Sect is still fighting the encirclement. The Orthodox Alliance Daily said they're sending a sizeable army to push back the Crimson Devils' advance."

"Okay?"

"...Read the document I'm sending you."

I reached for the qi-phone.

Then I attached a small red-bar-shaped device to its side.

It was less than three centimeters long.

The screen flickered.

For a moment, nothing happened.

Then a strange red-black bubble appeared inside our chat.

Placeholder stared at it.

"What is that?"

"A privacy device."

"Where did you get it?"

"Old Chen."

"..."

"He's got connections."

I didn't elaborate.

The device encrypted the connection and obscured both the sender and receiver.

Officially, things like this were illegal.

Unofficially...

Everyone knew they existed.

The higher-ups probably knew too.

They just couldn't stop every whisper in the sect.

I sent the file.

It was a copy of an issue from an independent news agency operating inside the Orthodox Alliance.

Old Chen had somehow gotten his hands on it.

I waited.

Placeholder's eyes moved across the screen.

Once.

Twice.

Then she stopped.

"..."

Her expression changed.

"Oh my..."

She looked at me.

"What...?"

Her voice became quieter.

"X-Xu..."

"Yeah."

"Is this r-real?"

"Yeah."

She stared at the screen again.

I leaned back.

"Don't say anything about this over the call."

She nodded.

"Okay."

There was a long silence.

Then she whispered:

"The Golden Talisman Sect..."

"Yeah."

"They..."

"Yeah."

I swallowed.

"The Golden Talisman Sect fell more than six months ago."

Placeholder's face went pale.

"They switched sides."

For a few seconds, neither of us said anything.

Six months.

That was how long we'd been hearing about the heroic resistance of the Golden Talisman Sect.

Six months of newspapers.

Six months of official reports.

Six months of talks about reinforcements.

Six months of preparing supplies.

And apparently...

We'd been preparing to help the wrong side.

I looked at the independent report again.

The implications were enormous.

If this was true, then the Golden Talisman Sect hadn't merely lost.

They had surrendered.

And then...

They had joined the enemy.

Which meant the "Crimson Devils' encirclement" we'd been hearing about wasn't the whole story.

Not even close.

I slowly looked at Placeholder.

"Well..."

She didn't respond.

I sighed.

"Our so called 'business' is not merely for monetary profit"
